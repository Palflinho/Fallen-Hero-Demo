# Fallen Hero — Free Demo

**A top-down action roguelite about four animal heroes, four elemental temples, and a moral choice with no clean answer.**

**[⬇ Download the latest demo](../../releases/latest)** · Windows (PC) and Android builds are attached to each release.

> **Status:** early Alpha. Everything you see and hear is a **placeholder**: characters are drawn with simple procedural shapes, and the temporary music was AI-generated only so we could test the game with sound. We're a small team and none of us are artists, so our goal is to fund real artists for the final art and audio.

---

## What's in the demo

The demo covers the **whole Water Temple**, from the village to its boss:

1. **Underground Village.** Pick your hero, buy talents with the gold you bring back, and choose 3 starting talents at the portal. You can also practice on the training dummies.
2. **Two labyrinths.** Generated on the spot for each run. Step on the 4 ancient seals to open the exit.
3. **Mini-boss:** the Temple Golem and its escort.
4. **Merchant:** potions, stat upgrades and talents.
5. **Third labyrinth,** where spellcasters and totems join the fight.
6. **Arena:** enemy waves, then Undine, the Water Spirit.
7. **Boss: the Water General.** Hit his ice orb back at him until he freezes, then strike.

It's a roguelite: dying sends you back to the village with part of your gold, and every run is different. Each hero plays differently, so try all 4.

The full game adds the Fire, Wind and Earth Temples, the Savior's Sanctuary, 16 elemental specializations and three endings.

## The heroes

| Hero | Class | Playstyle |
|---|---|---|
| **Rhino** | Knight | Tank. Blocks attacks with a shield and can stop charging enemies. |
| **Fox** | Mage | Control and artillery. Interrupts enemy spells and channels. |
| **Tegu Lizard** | Archer | Precision at range. Shoots down flying enemies. |
| **Urutau** | Assassin | Stealth and critical hits from behind. |

## How it plays

- **Readable combat.** Every enemy telegraphs its attack with a "!", a closing ring and an arrow. When the ring turns white, block or dodge for a perfect defense.
- **Elemental Power.** Hitting, blocking and dodging fill your Essence bar, and perfect blocks and dodges fill it faster. When it's full, **hold attack** to charge a large area attack, then release.
- **Talents.** 5 slots per run: 3 you pick before leaving and 2 you find along the way. You can discard up to 5 talents per run so they stop showing up.

## Controls

| Action | Keyboard | Gamepad |
|---|---|---|
| Move | WASD / Arrows | Left stick / D-Pad |
| Attack | Z | A / Cross |
| Elemental Power (full Essence) | Hold Z, release | Hold A, release |
| Defend / Special | X / K | X / Square |
| Hero sheet & talents / Pause | T / Esc | Start |
| Options (while paused) | O | R1 |
| Portal: choose talent / start run | Z / Space | A / Start |

Portuguese and English are available in Options. The Android build uses on-screen touch controls.
