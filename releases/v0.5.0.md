# Fallen Hero v0.5.0 Alpha — Demo

The biggest update so far for the **Fallen Hero** demo, a top-down action roguelite about four animal heroes, four elemental temples and a moral choice with no clean answer. The demo still covers the whole **Water Temple**. Its labyrinths are now explored under a fog of war and have their own water puzzles, and combat gained a 3-hit combo and 50 new talents.

> **Heads-up:** art and audio are still placeholders. Characters are simple procedural shapes, and the temporary music was AI-generated just so we could test with sound. The final art and soundtrack will be made by artists we hire.

## New: Exploration
- **Labyrinths are built on a 4×4 grid with missing cells**, so each one has **12 to 15 rooms** and a different shape every run. Rooms are smaller and more focused.
- **Fog of war.** Only the room you're in is lit. Rooms you've visited stay dimmed, and the rest are pitch black until you walk in. The minimap only shows what you've explored, and only the monsters in your current room.
- **Phase items.** Each labyrinth hides 2 of these 4 items. Clear the room guarding one and it drops:
  - **Map:** reveals the whole layout.
  - **Compass:** marks the seals, the exit, doors and chests.
  - **Radar:** shows every monster on the map.
  - **Lens:** shows monster health, rarity, elite affix and weak spot. Without it, that information stays hidden in labyrinths.
- **Room-clear bonus.** Defeating every monster in a room rewards gold, Essence and a chance of a chest.
- **Run outside combat.** With no enemies nearby, your hero speeds up on their own.

## New: Water puzzles
Each labyrinth can swap its 4 seals for a water objective and add a puzzle to its treasure room. There are never more than 2 per labyrinth, and the first labyrinth keeps the classic seals.
- **River Flow:** break the debris damming the river.
- **Fill the Altar:** carry water from 4 pedestals to the altar.
- **Rebuild the Bridge:** carry 4 planks to the broken bridge.
- **Key Thief:** a slippery water mob runs off with the exit key and leaves a slowing trail.
- **Treasure puzzles:** Build the Bridge (push crates into the current), Water Jets (close the valves) and Flood (open the sluices).

When you carry items, **every hit you take makes you drop one**, so the monsters around matter. The Fire, Wind and Earth temples have puzzles of their own in the full game.

## Combat
- **3-hit combo.** Keep attacking in rhythm to chain hits. The 3rd hit is a **finisher**: more damage, more knockback and a class-specific effect.
  - **Knight:** a much wider sweep that marks the target with the element.
  - **Assassin:** a short lunge and a guaranteed critical hit.
  - **Archer and Mage:** a bigger, faster projectile that pierces one more enemy.
- **Each specialization's signature move** (Tidal Surge, Magma Meteor, Seismic Rupture, Frost Volley...) now fires on the combo finisher.
- **Livelier attacks:** projectile trails in your element's color, slash arcs on melee swings, shockwaves on area attacks and impact rings on every hit.
- **Elemental Power range is shown while charging:** the blast circle, or the Assassin's dash path.
- Melee classes deal **15% more damage**, and ranged classes deal **10% less**.
- **Melee guard** (shield, Parry, Aegis, Obsidian Skin) only goes on cooldown if it actually blocks something.
- **Space no longer defends.** It's only for confirming and interacting; defend stays on X / K.

## Talents
- **50 new talents:**
  - **Elemental Power:** more Essence, faster charging, and a blast that heals, burns, summons cyclones or raises pillars.
  - **Class Echoes:** borrow another class's verb, such as holding charges, interrupting, shooting down flyers or striking from behind.
  - **Elemental Echoes:** a weak version of another element's signature.
  - **Combo talents:** a wider rhythm window, a stronger opener, finisher effects, and a legendary that turns the combo into 4 hits.
- Talents that work for any class show up in every tab of the Rift Emporium. Buy once and they unlock for all classes.
- **The 3 talents you pick at the portal start active** (rank 1). Inactive talents are clearly marked.
