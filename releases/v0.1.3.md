# Fallen Hero v0.1.3 Alpha — Demo

First public demo of **Fallen Hero**, a top-down action roguelite about four animal heroes, four elemental temples and a moral choice with no clean answer. The demo covers the whole **Water Temple**, from the village to its boss. The changes below came from our first private playtest.

> **Heads-up:** art and audio are placeholders. Characters are simple procedural shapes, and the temporary music was AI-generated just so we could test with sound. The final art and soundtrack will be made by artists we hire.

## New: Elemental Power
- An **Essence bar** fills as you fight. Hits and blocks fill it a little; **dodges** and **perfect blocks** fill it much faster.
- When the bar is full, **hold attack** to charge. You move slower while charging. **Release** for a large area attack themed by your element:
  - **Tidal Wave** (Water) slows and pushes enemies away.
  - **Eruption** (Fire) burns.
  - **Hurricane** (Wind) pulls enemies to the center.
  - **Earthquake** (Earth) stuns.
- **Each class uses it differently:** the Knight bursts around himself, the Mage and Archer strike ahead of them, and the Assassin dashes through everything in the way.

## Combat
- **Every enemy now telegraphs its attacks** with a "!", a closing ring and an arrow. When the ring turns white, block or dodge for a perfect defense.
- Attacks now have a minimum wind-up, so there is always time to react.
- Harder enemies: more health, more damage, faster attacks and more elites. Bosses are unchanged.
- The Water Knight and Water Assassin now use a short delayed water thrust instead of long-range blades, so they still feel like melee classes.

## Talents
- **Any talent of your class and element can drop during a run**, whether you bought it or not. Buying a talent in the village lets you **start** a run with it.
- **Discard up to 5 talents per run.** A discarded talent won't show up again in that run.
- Talent point costs are much lower (divided by 3).
- The chest screen shows what each equipped talent does, so you can compare.
- The "points available" alert only appears when you can actually spend them, and it goes away once you open the talent sheet.
- Changing class or element clears starting talents that no longer fit.

## Interface & audio
- **Options are available in-game** from the pause menu (O / R1).
- Portal controls: **Z** picks a talent and **Space** starts the run.
- The HUD fades when your hero or enemies are underneath it.
- Placeholder music for the village, each temple and the final boss.

## Fixes
- Bosses could get stuck at their first phase and stop taking damage.
- The blue barrier of Bulwark elites now disappears when it breaks.
- The Start or Esc button no longer opens the pause menu while a village window is open.
